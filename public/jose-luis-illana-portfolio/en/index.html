José Luis Illana Ruiz

Distributed Systems & Observability Architect | Senior Software Engineer | Banking & Regulated Environments

Software architect and senior software engineer with professional experience since 2008. He specializes in distributed systems, observability architecture, and monitoring platforms for banking and regulated environments.

He currently works as Principal Platform Architect (Technical Lead) at BBVA Technology, leading cross-functional technical decisions for Monitoring & Observability Platform BBVA. His work covers architecture, quality, and the evolution of cloud-native and on-premises platforms, with a focus on resilience, scalability, high availability, and the operational efficiency of critical banking services.

Selected Experience

In his current role, he defines horizontally scalable distributed architectures for high-volume, real-time ingestion and contributes to decisions involving Go backends, Elasticsearch, time-series platforms, and infrastructure. He also establishes secure-communication standards, governs delivery and quality practices, performs final technical validation before production, and leads critical-incident resolution and resilience improvements.

The platform processes more than one million ingestion operations per second in real time, combining individual requests with bulk loads containing hundreds or thousands of items.

He previously worked as a Big Data and Backend Developer at BEEVA—now BBVA Technology—contributing to Big Data architecture and development with Java and Scala. His projects included Reporting Operacional, an internal BBVA observability and reporting platform for banking procedures, and several proofs of concept for external clients, including Ferrovial.
